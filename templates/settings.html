What this app can and cannot do

  • Works with no paid service: starter list, GitHub public search, pages you paste, TXT/CSV import, manual entry, quality checks, submissions to permitted forms, backlink verification, outreach drafts, exports.
  • Needs something you provide: broad web search needs a SearXNG instance URL (below). Without it, discovery is limited to the other sources.
  • Never done automatically: CAPTCHA solving, logins, account creation, comment or forum posting, sending email.
  • Site-specific integrations: none are built in. Generic forms are handled; per-domain rules live in data/submissions.json.
